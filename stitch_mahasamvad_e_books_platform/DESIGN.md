## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, dignified, and serene digital reading environment for the Directorate General of Information and Public Relations (DGIPR), Government of Maharashtra. Merging the solemnity of official gazettes with the tactile warmth of archival print literature, the visual narrative evokes an institutional library preserving state heritage, public awareness periodicals, and historic documentation.

### Visual Philosophy & Influence
- **Contemporary Archival Editorial:** Heavy emphasis on paper-like substrate tones, refined hairline structural borders, and generous typographical vertical cadence designed specifically to respect complex Devanagari ligatures and matras.
- **Dignified Restraint:** Moving away from utilitarian bureaucratic interfaces toward a calm, prestige-driven digital e-reader. Visual hierarchy is established via typographic weight, ink tints, and warm tonal fields rather than harsh saturated fills or heavy drop shadows.
- **Physical Literary Cues:** Digital ephemera borrows tactility from physical publishing: book jackets adhere strictly to a 3:4 portrait ratio, complete with an inset inner spine shadow along the left edge, deckled separation dividers, and warm-white paper containers.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the contrast between physical newsprint/parchment surfaces and carbon printing ink, energized by ceremonial state colors:

- **State Crimson (`#A8172C`)**: Used as the primary brand accent for call-to-actions, primary progress states, chapter bookmarks, and active navigation indicators. Its companion soft wash (`#F7E6E8`) serves as a low-distraction interactive hover or selected state.
- **Muted Saffron-Gold (`#B7862C`)**: The secondary accent designated for editorial honors, official state archival tags, featured releases, and "नवीन" (New) markers, paired with `#F8EDD5` for surface pills.
- **Substrate Architecture**: The viewport foundation sits on `#F3F1EC` (Canvas), while reader panes and reading sheets occupy `#FBFAF7` (Paper). Raised modular components utilize `#FFFFFF` (Surface), with recessed elements (search bars, sliders, code blocks) sunken into `#EBE8E1`.
- **Ink Gradation**: Text legibility eschews synthetic pure black (`#000000`) in favor of rich press ink (`#17161A`), cascading into deliberate hierarchies: `#3A3833` for secondary context, `#6D6860` for metadata and timestamps, and `#9C968C` for decorative dividers and disabled indicators.

## Typography

The typographical engine supports bilingual parity (Marathi and English). In production environments, headline serifs prioritize `'Tiro Devanagari Marathi'` with fallback to `'Noto Serif'`, long-form body reading relies on `'Noto Serif Devanagari'`, and UI controls execute in `'Mukta'` or `'Public Sans'`.

### Devanagari Accommodations
- **Line Height Multipliers:** Devanagari's upper *matras* (velanti, matra) and lower markers (ukar, rasva) demand generous vertical margins. Line height tokens are locked strictly between **1.6 and 1.8** of font size to completely eradicate visual clipping across browsers.
- **Reading Rhythm:** The body reading viewport strictly constraints text width between 60 to 75 characters (approx. 680px) to mirror traditional royal gazette margins and minimize ocular fatigue during extended reading sessions.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows an editorial grid system inspired by broadsheet proportions:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 1200px maximum reading container width. Margins set to `margin` (3rem) with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters between publication columns. Multi-column catalog displays adapt to 3 or 4 book covers per row.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid scaling with 24px margins and 16px gutters. Reader mode expands to a single centered column flanked by collapsible reading toolbars.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Outer padding contracts to 16px (`1rem`). Catalog reflows to a structured 2-column book grid or full-width horizontal publication list items.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** All module margins and section dividers conform to an 8px atomic base grid, ensuring baseline alignment between multi-column columns and catalog cards.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain a crisp, high-end editorial identity, heavy blurs and dramatic drop shadows are strictly prohibited. Depth is rendered through structural layering and physical bookbinding techniques:

- **Hairline Boundary:** Surfaces sit one level above their background separated by a crisp `1px solid #E3DFD6` border. Active, hovered, or focused containers transition to `#CDC7BB`.
- **Book Spine Illusion:** Book covers feature an asymmetric left-edge gradient overlay: `linear-gradient(to right, rgba(0,0,0,0.18) 0px, rgba(0,0,0,0.06) 6px, transparent 16px)` to reproduce the physical indent of a bound spine without obscuring title art.
- **Ambient Sheet Elevation:** Floating controls (such as the persistent bottom e-reader toolbar or drawer menus) utilize a single atmospheric shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(23, 22, 26, 0.06)`, framed with the standard hairline border.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern radii:

- **Structural Containers & Reading Panes:** Standardized at 10px to 14px (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`) to soften structured government publishing without feeling overly casual or childish.
- **Category & Taxonomy Chips:** Expressed as fully rounded pills (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to establish immediate visual differentiation from square editorial cards.
- **Book Covers:** Hardbound finish with a subtle 4px to 6px outer corner radius, preserving the crisp rectilinearity of printed books.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `#A8172C`, text in `#FFFFFF`, with 12px border radius. Padding: 12px 24px (minimum height 48px to satisfy the accessibility touch standard). Hover switches to deep crimson `#8F1224`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent or `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CDC7BB`, text in `#17161A`. Hover shifts background to `#F3F1EC`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Text `#A8172C`, transparent background, 8px padding with an underline on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Badges ('नवीन' / 'विशेष'):** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), background `#F8EDD5`, text `#B7862C`, border `1px solid rgba(183, 134, 44, 0.25)`. Font: `label-sm`.
- **Filter Chips:** Pill-shaped, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E3DFD6`, text `#3A3833`. Active state switches to background `#F7E6E8`, text `#A8172C`, and border `#A8172C`.

### Book Cover & Catalog Card
- **Cover Display:** Exact 3:4 portrait aspect ratio. Encased in a `1px solid rgba(0,0,0,0.08)` outline. Spine shadow placed on the inner left edge.
- **Metadata Section:** Set on `#FFFFFF` with title in `headline-sm` (`Noto Serif`), author and date in `body-sm` (`#6D6860`).

### Reader Viewport Controls
- **Docked Reading Palette:** Centered floating island at the screen bottom, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E3DFD6`, shadow `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(23, 22, 26, 0.06)`. Houses font sizing controls (`अ / A`), theme selector, and chapter scrubber.
- **Inputs & Search:** Height 48px, background `#EBE8E1`, border `1px solid transparent`, corner radius 12px. Focused state activates background `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #A8172C`.
